Research Information Sheet

You are being invited to take part in a research study. Please read through the following information carefully before proceeding to the consent form.

What should I know about this research?

  • This form sums up the explanation of this research.
  • Taking part in this research is voluntary. Whether you take part is up to you.
  • You can choose not to take part. There will be no penalty.
  • You can agree to take part and later change your mind. There will be no penalty.
  • If you don’t understand, ask questions.
  • Ask all the questions you want before you decide.
  • There are attention check questions to see whether a participant has paid attention throughout the survey. Failing attention check may result in removal from this research and will not receive compensation.
  • This research collects your demographic profile (e.g., age, gender, ethnicity) but personally identifiable information (e.g., name, address, phone number) will not be asked.

Why is this research being done?

The major purpose of this research is to explore the impact of AI nudges in tourism and hospitality industry. While we cannot fully disclose the purpose of this research because it would negatively impact the scientific validity of this study, we are providing you with as much information as we can disclose without compromising the results.
